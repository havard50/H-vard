Short bio

Håvard Pedersen (Vardø / Oslo) — Gretsch-endorsed Norwegian guitarist, singer and songwriter. Guitar-driven blues rock. Live act: Håvard Pedersen & The Blues Is Alright Band. Studio: Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound.

Full bio

From the Arctic town of Vardø, Håvard Pedersen is a Norwegian guitarist, singer and songwriter rooted in blues rock — expressive tone, strong songs, and a live band that plays for real. Endorsed by Gretsch, he has recorded with guests including John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø, and works with producer Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound, Mjøndalen.

His catalogue includes albums, singles and new material — among them Never Felt So Good (2011), You and I (2021) and the single Det sku bli oss (2025). On stage he fronts Håvard Pedersen & The Blues Is Alright Band — guitar-driven, international live experience, no playback.
