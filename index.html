About Håvard

Power Trio. Rått. Ærlig. Live.

From Vardø in Arctic Norway, Håvard Pedersen is a guitarist, singer and songwriter rooted in blues-rock — expressive tone, strong songs, and shows built around real musicians playing live. Endorsed by Gretsch, he has recorded with John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø, and works with producer Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound.

On stage in 2026 he leads the Håvard Pedersen Trio — a guitar-driven power trio for clubs, festivals and concert halls. Albums include Never Felt So Good, You and I and the single Det sku bli oss.
