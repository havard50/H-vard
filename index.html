THE ARTIST

From the north.
From the heart.

From Vardø in Arctic Norway to the stages of Oslo, Håvard Pedersen brings expressive guitar playing, personal songs and a deep love of blues and rock.

A Gretsch-endorsed artist, he has recorded with John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø, and works with Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound.

On stage, the trio keeps it direct: guitar, bass, drums — and the connection with the room.
