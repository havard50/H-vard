THE LIVE SOUND

One guitar.
Three musicians.
A room full of blues.

Håvard’s live work spans solo performances and band concerts. His earlier trio performances include Wild Willy Bendiksen on drums and Knut Evenmo on bass and vocals.

Original material meets a repertoire rooted in Gary Moore, B.B. King, Stevie Ray Vaughan and Thin Lizzy.

THE ARTIST

From Vardø.
Out into the world.

Born in Vardø and based in Oslo, Håvard has taken his guitar to stages in the United States, England and across Europe. His touring story includes The Norwegian Beatles, Cinematic World and Three Men Electric Jam.

His recordings include collaborations with John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø. With producer Peter Michelsen of Donkeyboy at The Norwegian Sound, he explores the meeting of electric guitar and melodic songwriting.

Gary Moore, B.B. King and George Harrison are among the influences named in his festival biography. A Gretsch guitar is a recurring part of that sound, from the studio to the stage.
