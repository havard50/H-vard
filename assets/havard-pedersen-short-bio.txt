HÅVARD PEDERSEN
Guitarist, singer and songwriter · Vardø / Oslo, Norway

Håvard Pedersen is a Norwegian guitarist, singer and songwriter from Vardø, based in Oslo. Rooted in blues and rock, his music combines expressive electric guitar with personal songwriting. His recent work includes songs in his own Vardø dialect.

His recordings include collaborations with John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø. He works with producer Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound, and his performing background includes The Norwegian Beatles, Cinematic World and Three Men Electric Jam.

He performs solo and with Håvard Pedersen Trio, featuring Wild Willy Bendiksen on drums and Knut Evenmo on bass and vocals.
