VARDØ / OSLO

His roots.
His own voice.

Håvard Pedersen is a guitarist, singer and songwriter from Vardø, based in Oslo. His work brings together blues, rock and personal songwriting, with recent songs written in his Vardø dialect.

His performing background includes The Norwegian Beatles, Cinematic World and Three Men Electric Jam, with concerts in the United States, England and Europe.

His recordings include collaborations with John Norum and Ronni Le Tekrø. He works with producer Peter Michelsen at The Norwegian Sound. His musical influences include Gary Moore, B.B. King and George Harrison.
